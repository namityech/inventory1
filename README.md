# BENEN INVENTORY
### Smart Inventory & Business Management System

**Smarter Stock. Better Business. Zero Waste.**

## 📌 About the Project

BENEN INVENTORY is a modern inventory and business management application designed to help businesses efficiently manage products, monitor stock levels, track product expiry dates, manage customers, process orders, and analyze business performance.

The system aims to reduce inventory losses, prevent stock shortages, improve business operations, and simplify inventory management through an organized and user-friendly interface.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify inventory and product management.
- Monitor product expiry dates and prevent expired stock sales.
- Reduce losses caused by expired or missing products.
- Manage customers, suppliers, and orders.
- Track sales, payments, and stock movements.
- Generate useful business reports and analytics.
- Improve inventory accuracy and business decision-making.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Add, edit, and delete products.
- Track product quantities and prices.
- Manage product categories and suppliers.
- Monitor incoming and outgoing stock.
- Track inventory valuation.

### ⏳ Expiry Management
- Track product batches and expiry dates.
- Identify products expiring within 7, 30, and 60 days.
- Identify expired products.
- Generate expiry warnings.
- Estimate potential losses from expired stock.

### 👥 Customer Management
- Register new customers.
- Update customer information.
- View customer profiles.
- Track customer purchase history.
- Monitor outstanding balances.

### 🛒 Orders & Sales Management
- Create and manage customer orders.
- Add multiple products to an order.
- Calculate order totals automatically.
- Track payments and outstanding balances.
- Generate invoices and receipts.
- Automatically update inventory after sales.

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- Total products and stock quantities.
- Low-stock notifications.
- Expiry alerts.
- Inventory valuation.
- Sales performance.
- Fast-moving and slow-moving products.
- Daily, weekly, and monthly reports.
